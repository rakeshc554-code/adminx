## 1. Introduction

Welcome to AdminX — your all-in-one business management app. This manual will help you get started and make the most of AdminX's features.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Installation

| Platform | Where to Download |
| :--- | :--- |
| Android | Google Play Store -> Search "AdminX" |
| iOS | Apple App Store -> Search "AdminX" |
| Web | Visit app.adminx.app |
| Desktop (Windows) | Download from adminx.app/download |
| Desktop (macOS) | Download from adminx.app/download |

### 2.2 Registration

1. Open the app.
2. Tap "Sign Up".
3. Enter your email and phone number.
4. Create a password.
5. Verify your phone number via OTP.
6. Set up your business profile (name, GST, address).
7. You're ready to go!

### 2.3 Login

- Enter your email/phone and password.
- Or use the "Login with Google" option (if enabled).
- For multi-device, log in with the same credentials to sync data.

## 3. Core Features

### 3.1 Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you an instant snapshot of your business:

- Total Sales: Revenue in the last 30 days.
- Outstanding Balance: Total amount owed by customers.
- Low Stock Alerts: Products below reorder level.
- Recent Activity: Latest transactions and invoices.
- Quick Actions: "New Invoice", "Add Customer", "Record Payment".

### 3.2 Customers (Khata)

#### Add a Customer
1. Go to Customers tab.
2. Tap the "+" button.
3. Enter name, phone, email (optional), address.
4. Save -> customer appears in your list.

#### Record a Transaction
1. Tap a customer -> Ledger.
2. Tap "+" or "Add Transaction".
3. Select Credit (money received) or Debit (money given/outstanding).
4. Enter amount and description.
5. Save -> balance updates instantly.

#### Send Payment Reminder
1. From the customer's ledger, tap "Send Reminder".
2. Choose WhatsApp or SMS.
3. The message is automatically drafted; you can edit it before sending.

### 3.3 Invoicing

#### Create an Invoice
1. Go to Invoices -> "+".
2. Select a customer (or create a new one).
3. Add items: search existing products or add custom items.
4. Specify quantity and price.
5. GST is auto-calculated based on your business settings.
6. Review total and tap "Create Invoice".

#### Share an Invoice
- After creating, tap "Share".
- Choose WhatsApp, Email, or SMS.
- The invoice will be sent as a PDF or link.

#### Recurring Invoices
- In the invoice creation screen, toggle "Recurring".
- Set frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and end date.
- Invoices will be generated automatically.

#### Progress Invoices (for services)
- Enable "Progress Invoicing" in settings.
- When creating an invoice, specify milestone percentage (e.g., 50%).
- The invoice will show progress and remaining balance.

### 3.4 Inventory

#### Add a Product
1. Go to Products -> "+".
2. Enter name, SKU (auto-generated or custom), price, cost (optional).
3. Set initial quantity and reorder level.
4. You can generate a barcode by tapping "Generate Barcode".
5. Save.

#### Scan Barcode
- In the invoice or product screen, tap the scan icon.
- Point your camera at the barcode.
- The product will be added automatically.

#### Low Stock Alerts
- When quantity falls below reorder level, a notification appears on the dashboard and as a push alert.

#### Expiry Tracking
- For products with expiry dates (e.g., food, medicine), enter the date.
- You'll receive an alert 30 days before expiry.

### 3.5 Expenses & Accounting

#### Record an Expense
1. Go to Expenses -> "+".
2. Enter amount, category (e.g., Rent, Utilities, Salaries), and description.
3. Optionally attach a receipt photo.
4. Save.

#### AI Auto-Categorization
- When you type a description, AI suggests a category.
- You can accept or change it.

#### Bank Reconciliation
1. Go to Accounting -> Reconciliation.
2. Upload your bank statement (CSV/Excel).
3. AdminX will match transactions automatically.
4. Unmatched ones are highlighted for manual review.

#### Cash Flow Forecast
- Available if you have at least 6 months of data.
- The AI predicts your income and expenses for the next 3 months.
- Helps you plan for lean periods.

#### Reserve Fund
1. Go to Settings -> Reserve Fund.
2. Set a target amount (e.g., Rs 50,000).
3. AdminX tracks contributions and restricts withdrawals until target is met.

### 3.6 Employees

#### Add Employee
1. Go to Employees -> "+".
2. Enter name, role (e.g., Sales, Admin), phone, email.
3. Assign permissions (e.g., view-only, edit, admin).

#### Track Attendance
- Employees can mark attendance from their login.
- View attendance reports by date or employee.

#### Payroll
1. Go to Payroll.
2. Select month.
3. AdminX calculates salaries based on attendance and salary data.
4. Generate payslips as PDFs.

### 3.7 Reports

#### Available Reports
- Dashboard (summary metrics)
- Profit & Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet
- GST Reports (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9)
- Sales Reports (by customer, product, date)
- Expense Reports (by category)

#### Export Reports
- On any report screen, tap "Export".
- Choose PDF (for sharing) or Excel/CSV (for analysis).

### 3.8 AI Chat Assistant

- Look for the AI chat icon (floating button).
- Tap to open the chat.
- Type questions like:
  - "How much is Ramesh's pending balance?"
  - "What were my sales last week?"
  - "Show my top 5 customers"
  - "When is the next GST filing due?"
- The AI uses your local data to answer instantly.
- Privacy guarantee: No data is sent to the cloud.

## 4. Multi-Device & Sync

- Login with the same credentials on all your devices (phone, tablet, desktop).
- Data is synced automatically when you're online.
- You can work offline — changes are queued and synced when you reconnect.

### Conflict Resolution
- If you edit the same customer on two devices, AdminX uses intelligent conflict resolution (CRDT) to merge changes without data loss.

---

## 5. Data Backup & Security

### Automatic Backup
- Your data is backed up daily to the cloud (encrypted).
- You can also manually create a backup: Settings -> Backup -> Create Backup.

### Restore Backup
- Settings -> Backup -> Restore.
- Select a backup from the list.
- Your current data will be replaced.

### Privacy
- All sensitive data is encrypted on your device.
- AI runs entirely on-device — your data never leaves your control.
- You can delete all your data at any time (Settings -> Delete Account).

## 7. Troubleshooting

### App Crashes
- Update to the latest version.
- Clear cache (Settings -> Clear Cache).
- If problem persists, contact support.

### Sync Issues
- Check internet connection.
- Force sync: Settings -> Sync -> Force Sync.
- Log out and log back in.

### AI Not Responding
- Ensure you have the AI model downloaded (Settings -> AI -> Model Download).
- For slower devices, some complex queries may take longer.

### Payment Reminders Not Sending
- Verify WhatsApp/SMS gateway settings (ask your admin).
- Ensure customer phone numbers are correct (with country code).
